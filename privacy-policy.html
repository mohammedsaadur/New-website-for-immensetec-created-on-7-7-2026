Privacy Policy

How Immensetec LLC collects, uses, and protects your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information for visitors, clients, candidates, and other users of our website and services.

Immensetec LLC is committed to protecting the privacy of all users who visit Immensetec.com (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information. By using this Website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

The terms "you" and "your" refer to all clients, visitors, candidates, and other users of our website, services, and products. The terms "we," "our," and "us" refer to Immensetec LLC, including our affiliates, subsidiaries, and employees.

Information We Collect

Immensetec LLC may collect personal information when you visit our Website, request services, or submit inquiries. The types of information we may collect include:

  • Personal Information: Details that identify you, such as your name, contact number, email address, or mailing address, which we collect when you interact with us.
  • Demographic Information: Data that reflects socioeconomic characteristics, such as age, zip code, and job history, helping us tailor services to your needs.
  • Behavioral Information: Data related to browsing habits, like pages visited or services accessed, which help us better understand and meet your interests.
  • Information from Third-Party Sources: We may gather data from job boards, social media, and public sites to enhance candidate profiles. By using our services, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of such information for potential job opportunities.

Methods of Collection

We use various methods to collect information on the Website:

  • Direct Collection: Information you voluntarily provide through sign-ups, queries, or participation in interactive Website features.
  • Cookies: Small files used to collect data such as IP address, browser type, and geographic location, allowing us to analyze preferences and improve user experience.
  • Web Beacons: Small images used to track activities on our Website, helping us understand and meet user needs.
  • Log Files: Records storing data like IP addresses and timestamps, which assist in analyzing preferences and enhancing our services.

How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used only for the purposes stated in this Policy, including:

  • To evaluate user needs and improve services.
  • To inform you of relevant job openings, after receiving your approval to share your profile with potential employers.
  • To update you on services, events, or promotions related to Immensetec.
  • To respond to queries, gather feedback, and enhance user experience.
  • To meet legal obligations and assist law enforcement when required.

By providing your contact information, you agree to receive updates on open positions and events at Immensetec LLC.

Disclosure of Personal Data

Except as required by law, we will not sell or share your information outside our organization. Information may be shared with our employees, affiliates, and partners when appropriate to meet your professional needs.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Data Security

Immensetec LLC takes steps to secure your personal data from unauthorized access, using measures such as Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology. While we strive to protect your data, no security system is foolproof. You are encouraged to protect your password and other login credentials.

Your Consent

By using our Website, you consent to the terms of this Privacy Policy, including our collection, use, and disclosure of information as outlined.

Communication Consent

By providing a telephone number, email address, and submitting a form on this Website, you are consenting to be contacted by SMS text message or email. You can reply STOP to opt out of further messaging.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Immensetec LLC may modify this Policy as needed. We encourage users to review it periodically for updates to ensure awareness of our data handling practices.
